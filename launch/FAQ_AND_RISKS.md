# EXIT AI — Public FAQ and risk disclosure (draft)

**What is EXIT AI?** A prototype Solana token discovery and simulated trading website.

**Is EXIT AI already autonomous?** No. The automatic-entry security gate blocks trades while on-chain security verification is incomplete.

**Is its token $EXIT live?** No official token address has been published or verified as part of this preparation.

**Does $EXIT provide ownership or revenue rights?** No such rights have been specified. Do not infer any rights or benefits from token ownership.

**Is AI research available?** The server has an optional AI endpoint; an API credential and validation are required. Screening scores alone are not AI.

**Can I lose money buying $EXIT?** Yes. Crypto tokens can lose all their value; liquidity, volatility, concentration, smart-contract, wallet and platform risks are material.

**Are returns guaranteed?** No. EXIT AI does not guarantee performance, prevent scams, or promise any trading result.

**Does the website place live orders?** No. Its trading features are simulated only.
